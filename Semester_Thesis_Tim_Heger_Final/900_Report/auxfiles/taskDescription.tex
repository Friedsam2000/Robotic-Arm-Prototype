Currently, the Chair of Automatic Control Theory lacks a lightweight and safe-to-handle robotic arm suitable for practical control experiments.
The existing prototype includes a MATLAB-based software suite with a basic velocity controller and simplistic trajectory planning.
This setup should be extended with more advanced control and planning algorithms, accompanied by theoretical considerations and evaluation.
The servo motors feature indirect torque measurement, enabling control methods such as torque control.
Ultimately, the goal is to establish a reliable and modular experimental platform for the exploration of robotic arm control strategies at the chair.
